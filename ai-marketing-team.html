    AI Marketing Team for Financial Advisors

    AI Systems, 2026

    Project Overview

    Managed end-to-end social media presence for two financial advisor clients. The work covered everything from research and ideation to design, scripting, motion graphics, Meta ads, and AI-powered automation, building a consistent and credible brand for both clients across Instagram and Facebook.

    The challenge with financial clients is trust. People don't follow financial advisors for entertainment. They follow for clarity, confidence, and credibility. Every post, reel, and ad was built with that in mind.

    How We Worked

    01

    Weekly research and ideation

    Every week started with research trending financial topics, news, audience pain points and turning that into a content plan for the week.

    02

    Script and caption writing

    Scripts written for short-form reels hook, body, CTA. Captions written with the right hashtags, keywords, and tone for financial audiences.

    03

    Design and motion graphics

    Static posts, carousels, story designs, animated intros, and motion graphics all created to match the brand identity of each client.

    04

    Visual generation with AI

    AI tools used to generate unique visuals, backgrounds, and creative assets that would have taken hours to produce manually.

    05

    Meta ads setup and management

    Ad creatives designed, copy written, campaigns set up and monitored targeting financial audiences on Facebook and Instagram.

    06

    Scheduling and posting

    Content published at optimal times across platforms. Consistency maintained across both client accounts every week.

    AI Agents Built for This

    Custom AI agents were set up to speed up the workflow without replacing the thinking. Each agent handled a specific part of the pipeline.

    Research, Ideation & Script Writing Agent
    Pulls weekly trending topics in personal finance and investment, turns them into content ideas for the week, and converts approved ideas into full reel scripts with a strong hook, clear body, and call to action. All tailored to the tone and positioning of each financial advisor client.
    Research Ideation Scripting
    Ad Copy Agent
    Handles SEO, keyword research, and competitor analysis to find what the target audience is searching for, then generates multiple versions of Meta ad copy with different hooks, CTAs, and angles for A/B testing. Built to follow financial compliance guidelines and avoid restricted language.
    Meta Ads SEO Keyword Research
    Visual Generation Agent
    Generates AI images and visual concepts for posts and reels backgrounds, illustrations, and abstract finance visuals reducing design time significantly.
