    AI Chatbot for Interior Design Company

    AI Systems, 2026

    Project Overview

    An interior design company was losing potential clients because their team couldn't respond to every inquiry fast enough. Interested customers had no way to share their requirements unless someone from the team was available to take a call.

    I built an AI chatbot that collects all the details the design team needs property type, dimensions, budget, style preferences and automatically schedules a consultation call with the team. The customer gets a response instantly, and the team gets a qualified lead with all the context ready.

    The Problem

    • Customers visiting the website had no way to share requirements instantly
    • Sales team was spending time on unqualified calls without knowing the budget or scope
    • Inquiries coming after business hours were lost
    • No structured way to collect property details before the first call

    How the Chatbot Works

    01

    Customer starts a chat on the website

    The bot greets the visitor and asks what they are looking to design home, office, or plot.

    02

    Collects property and requirement details

    It asks for property size, number of rooms, location, and any specific style preferences the customer has in mind.

    03

    Understands the budget range

    The bot asks for the customer's budget in a conversational way no forms, no dropdowns, just a natural question.

    04

    Schedules a call with the design team

    Based on availability, the bot books a consultation call and sends a confirmation. The team receives a full summary of the customer's requirements before the call.
