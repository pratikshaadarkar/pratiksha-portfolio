About Pratiksha

Who I Am

My journey started during school camps where I discovered my love for writing stories and sometimes poems. As I grew up, I became interested in filmmaking and started making short films during college.

At that time, hiring an editor was too expensive, so I learned video editing on my own. Soon, I was editing not only my own films but also freelance projects. Along the way, I learned writing, shooting, editing, production, and got the chance to work on tv and film sets.

Later, a friend and I started TOBC Entertainment, where we worked on creative projects for brands. That's when I realized that creating good content is only one part of the job. It also needs the right strategy to reach the right people. This led me to learn marketing, social media, and personal branding.

Today, I help businesses grow through marketing, content, and AI. Just like I taught myself editing years ago, I'm now learning and using AI to build better systems, create better content, and help brands grow in smarter ways.
